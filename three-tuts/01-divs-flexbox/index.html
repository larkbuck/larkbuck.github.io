Lesbian Detective

The truth is I am not a lesbian. But what is truth anyway? Just a chase. Factually, I am not a lesbian since I date a trans-masculine person. Since they don't identify as a woman (nor do I), my specific identity does not fall into the category of lesbianism. I might as well be a Queer Nonbinary Pansexual AFAB Detective... But let's be real: we can get lost in specificity. THAT is the #1 rule in detective work — stay tuned with specifics, but don't lose track of the bigger picture.
