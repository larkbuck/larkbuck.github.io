Bio

I like to play. I'll play in all kinds of ways. I used to play with dirt in flowerbeds, thick with nasturtiums. "Nasties" my grandma called them. She loved everything sinful and dirty, strutting it as a silken robe. Her name was Eunice. She taught me to love the edges that refused to fit within the form.

When I was young I wanted to be a veterinarian but couldn't pronounce it. "Vegetarian" I would say. And then I was a vegetarian for a very long time.

I teach now, a bonafide adjunct professor. They call us 'lecturers' in academia - those of us without the seal of tenure. I don't mind it, the relative lack of prestige. I teach fully and live the rest of my life beyond the university. I sleep well and go dancing often.
